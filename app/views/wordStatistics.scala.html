How words are counted

Counts combine all post languages. Case and equivalent Unicode spellings are normalized; accents are retained. Straight and curly internal apostrophes are treated alike.

URLs, numbers, and emoji are excluded. Hyphens and other separators split words; letters in hashtags and mentions still count. Common words such as “the” and “and” are included.

Words are letter sequences, so these counts do not provide language-specific segmentation for scripts without spaces. Ties use Unicode string order rather than a language-specific dictionary.
