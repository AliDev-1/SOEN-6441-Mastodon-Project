A higher Reading Ease score suggests easier reading. Grade Level estimates a US school grade.

These are rough English-text estimates, especially for short posts. Scores are not capped and can fall outside the usual ranges.

How these estimates are calculated

Reading Ease = 206.835 − 1.015 × (words ÷ sentences) − 84.6 × (syllables ÷ words).

Grade Level = 0.39 × (words ÷ sentences) + 11.8 × (syllables ÷ words) − 15.59.

URLs are excluded. Words are letter sequences with optional internal apostrophes. Periods, question marks, and exclamation marks separate sentences; a fragment with words counts as a sentence.

Syllables are estimated from vowel groups with a silent-e adjustment. Abbreviations, names, acronyms, and unusual pronunciations can reduce accuracy.
